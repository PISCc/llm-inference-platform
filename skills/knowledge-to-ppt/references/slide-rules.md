# Slide rules

## Narrative

- Define one communication job for the whole deck.
- Give each slide one claim and make adjacent slides answer successive questions.
- Use the title slide to state the topic and audience outcome, not an agenda.
- End with a synthesis, decision boundary, or concrete next step.

## Copy limits

- Deck title: at most 32 Chinese characters.
- Slide title: at most 26 Chinese characters and one line.
- Takeaway: at most 70 Chinese characters.
- Bullets: at most five; each at most 50 Chinese characters.
- Shorten copy before reducing type.

## Layout and typography

- Use 1280 × 720 (16:9).
- Use at least 52 px for the deck title, 38 px for slide titles, 24 px for subheads, and 18 px for body copy.
- Keep equal left and right margins. Prefer a flat composition over dashboard-style card grids.
- Use the platform theme by default: warm neutral canvas, charcoal text, cyan primary accent, violet/emerald/amber secondary accents.
- Use native shapes only for simple structure or a diagram that materially improves comprehension.

## Safety and provenance

- Preserve formulas, qualifiers, and uncertainty exactly.
- Do not infer measured performance from structural counts or capacity formulas.
- In diagnosis decks, label causes as candidates and keep evidence, inference, and validation steps distinct.
- Put `[Sources]` in every slide's speaker notes. List page context and knowledge entries used for that slide.
